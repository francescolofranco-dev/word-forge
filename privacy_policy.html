WordForge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

WordForge is a vocabulary learning app that runs entirely on your device. WordForge does not collect your data or send it to a server.

Data Storage

All data you enter into WordForge — words, meanings, progress, and statistics — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy app data according to your device settings.

Data Collection

WordForge does not collect any personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal data.

Network Access

WordForge works offline and does not request the Internet permission. It does not send your vocabulary, progress, or statistics to a server.

Third-Party Services

WordForge does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Where available, the app requests fonts through the Google Play services font provider on your device. System fonts are used when that provider is unavailable.

Permissions

WordForge requests the following permission:

Notifications: Used to send you word review reminders at scheduled times. This permission is optional — the app works without it, but you won't receive reminders. No notification data is sent to any server.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual words or all words at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Android backups are managed through your device's backup settings.

Children's Privacy

WordForge does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Since WordForge collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
